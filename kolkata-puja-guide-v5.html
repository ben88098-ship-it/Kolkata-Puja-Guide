🛺 Autos
  • Shared autos run fixed routes from outside stations, about ₹10–30 a head. Ask "[place] jabe?" first.
  • Reserved autos have no meter. Fix the fare before sitting; expect more after 9 pm Saptami–Navami.
  • Uber/Ola/Rapido surge near big pandals. Walk 500 m away first.
  • Police ban vehicles near big pandals in the evening; get dropped at the nearest open point.
🚇 Metro tickets & changes
  • Use a smart card or the QR ticket in the Aamar Kolkata Metro app; fares are distance-based and low.
  • Esplanade: Blue ↔ Green. Noapara: Blue ↔ Yellow. Dum Dum: rail link. Kavi Subhash: Orange Line.
  • Big stations may use one-way crowd flow; follow staff.
⏰ Best timing
  • Calmest: 8–11 am, or after 1 am on night-long days.
  • Worst: 7–11 pm Saptami–Navami, especially Ashtami (Sunday).
  • Do north or south in one day, not both. Plan 2–3 pandals an hour at most.
🍛 Food
  • Phuchka, ghugni, kathi rolls (New Market / Esplanade area).
  • Many pandals serve bhog (khichuri) on Ashtami and Navami.
  • Use restrooms at malls or restaurants before joining a long queue.
🌊 Dashami (20 Oct)
  • Sindoor khela, then immersion processions near Ganga ghats. Strand Road and riverside areas jam by evening. Check the metro app for that day's timings.

Before you leave

Stay safe

  • Police help desks are at big pandals and major stations.
  • Zip valuables; pickpockets work queues.
  • If a lane is too packed, step out. Don't push with kids or elders.
  • Share live location on late-night hops.
